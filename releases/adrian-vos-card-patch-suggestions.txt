WORKSHOP SYSTEM PATCH NOTES — ADRIAN VOS CARDS

These are starting patches designed to be entered and saved in Vincent Maurer's
Patch Notes tool: record each cable, then set knobs and switches to taste.
Levels are deliberately conservative; bring feedback, resonance, drive, and
external modular signals up slowly. “Clock”, “pitch”, “gate”, and “modulation”
refer to any compatible Workshop System source.

====================================================================
101 — C1ZZL3 GNARLY: CLOCKED INDUSTRIAL LEAD
====================================================================
Patch: clock/gate source → C1ZZL3 gate or trigger input; pitch sequencer →
pitch CV input; C1ZZL3 audio outs → stereo mixer. Send a slow random CV to one
tone/modulation input. Start with a short sequence, low resonance, and modest
drive; raise the random CV amount until every few notes have a different edge.

118 — JP8K-SANDSQUALL: WIDE CLOCKED STING
====================================================================
Patch: master clock → sequencer clock input; JP8K stereo outs → mixer. In its
sequencer mode, start the internal phrase, set spread around the middle and
brightness slightly below it. Use a slow envelope or random CV on brightness.
For a second version, take pitch CV and gate from a sequencer instead, and use
the card as a played supersaw voice.

174 — SKIPPING STONES: SELF-GENERATING HATS AND MELODY
====================================================================
Patch: Pulse Out 1 → drum/envelope trigger; Pulse Out 2 → second percussion
trigger; CV Out 1 → 1V/oct oscillator; CV Out 2 → filter cutoff; oscillator →
filter/VCA → mixer. Start in Sparse with medium Deja Vu and low Spread. Raise
Spread for variation, then raise Deja Vu to make a happy accident recur.

197 — COSMIK M1N1: EXTERNAL OSC3 BASS DIALOGUE
====================================================================
Patch: pitch sequencer → pitch CV; gate source → gate input; external oscillator
→ OSC3/audio return; Cosmik audio out → mixer. Begin with low-pass filter half
open, low resonance, short contour, and slight OSC3 level. Detune the external
oscillator a little, then open the filter on accents with an envelope or slow CV.

202 — SCARYLINGO: RHYTHMIC RING-MOD DRUM BUS
====================================================================
Patch: drum loop → Audio In 1; slow oscillator or sequencer voice → Audio In 2;
ScaryLingo out → mixer/effect return. Set mix near dry at first and introduce
ring modulation gradually. Clock a stepped CV into carrier pitch or mix, then
use the LFO page for a slow sine-to-square movement across the loop.

29 — XHT CARD: CHORD SWARM THROUGH SLOW MOVEMENT
====================================================================
Patch: pitch CV → pitch input; gate/clock → trigger input if used; XHT stereo
outs → mixer. Send one slow triangle LFO to a timbre/shape CV input and a much
slower random CV to a second modulation input. Start with a stable chord and
low movement; make the random modulation just audible, not fully chaotic.

34 — DUALQUANT: QUANTISED GRANULAR FEEDBACK VOICE
====================================================================
Patch: melodic source or voice → audio input; pitch sequence → pitch CV;
DualQuant outputs → stereo mixer. If using its calibrated CV outputs, send one
to an oscillator pitch input and the other to a filter or second oscillator.
Start with a sparse melody, small granular shift, and short feedback; increase
the shift in semitone-like steps for a call-and-response line.

433 — 433 SENSE OF SPACE: RESTLESS QUIET ROOM
====================================================================
Patch: Pulse Out from a slow clock or manual trigger → restart input; 433 stereo
outs → mixer → long, quiet reverb. Keep the card's own level restrained and let
the restlessness/fidget controls make rare events. The Mayakovsky CC0 edition
uses the same patch; substitute it when its theatre ambience suits the piece.

43 — CASTLE PROCESS: GATED COLLAPSE
====================================================================
Patch: drum machine, guitar, or oscillator → audio input; envelope or gate →
control/trigger input; Castle Process out → mixer. Begin with the chopping and
feedback low. Use a sparse gate pattern to make rhythmic cuts, then briefly
increase the harshness or feedback at phrase endings before returning to safety.

49 — TAPE BIAS: RECORDED PHRASE WITH UNSTABLE RECALL
====================================================================
Patch: voice, percussion, or field recording → audio input; trigger/clock →
record or transport trigger if desired; Tape Bias out → mixer. Record a short
four- to eight-beat phrase, then alter virtual bias slightly while replaying.
Use slow CV only sparingly: the charm is a recognisable loop becoming imperfect.

52 — F1SHNET: ENVELOPE-FOLLOWING FILTER PERCUSSION
====================================================================
Patch: drum loop or plucked oscillator → audio input; F1shnet out → mixer.
Optionally patch a clock or random CV to its sample-and-hold/control input.
Set the envelope follower for a clear response to each hit, then introduce
stepped cutoff changes at a slower division than the drums for a playful groove.

54 — TAPEGRADE: UNEVEN STEREO MEMORY
====================================================================
Patch: mono synth, drum loop, or voice → mono input; stereo outs → two mixer
channels. Start with almost no wow/flutter and moderate dry/wet balance. Add a
very slow triangle CV to a movement control, then bring in hiss only until it
appears in gaps. Automate the wet level for transitions rather than leaving it
fully wet.

580 — VORTEX RUNNER: SPLIT-VOICE CINEMATIC INTERVAL
====================================================================
Patch: pitch CV or MIDI → Vortex Runner; gate source → gate input; Audio Out 1
→ mixer channel A; Audio Out 2 → mixer channel B. Keep Voice A centered and
Voice B detuned slightly. Give Voice B a darker filter and slower LFO, then pan
the mixer channels apart. A long gate pattern makes the shared articulation clear.

58 — LOCHO VIBES: SLOW STEREO LIQUID CHORD
====================================================================
Patch: chord source → audio input; LoCho Vibes stereo outs → mixer. Begin in
chorus with a slow sine or triangle LFO, modest depth, and even stereo balance.
For a transition, increase depth and slow the rate before moving toward vibrato;
return to chorus before the next rhythmic section.

59 — BITPHASE: RESONANT MOTION WITHOUT LOSING THE BEAT
====================================================================
Patch: drum bus, bass, or sustained voice → audio input; BitPhase out → mixer.
Clock an envelope or stepped CV at one-half or one-quarter of the rhythm into a
phase/modulation control. Keep resonance below self-oscillation, use tremolo as
an accent layer, and sweep manually for a single highlighted fill.

74 — WILD PEBBLE: GENERATIVE DUO
====================================================================
Patch: MIDI clock or master clock → Wild Pebble clock; its melodic CV →
oscillator pitch; its gate/pulse → envelope or drum trigger; oscillator → mixer.
Route a second output to filter cutoff or a second percussion voice. Start with
a repeating, low-complexity pattern; loosen the generative controls gradually so
the patch stays related to the original motif.

77 — PUNK CONFUSION: APC TO BROKEN-VENUE DROP
====================================================================
Patch A: Punk Confusion audio out → mixer; gate source → Pulse In 1 for APC
rhythms. Patch B: drum loop or guitar/synth → Audio In 1; output → mixer.
Begin in APC mode with a simple gate rhythm. On a transition, switch to Broken
Venue and feed the external source, using a short burst of feedback/room colour;
use a Down vocal stab only as a punctuation mark.

818 — BIB: DUB SEND WITH MOVING TAPE
====================================================================
Patch: stereo drum loop or two related mono sources → Bib inputs; Bib stereo
outs → mixer return. Send slow CV to the two performance controls. Start with
short delay, a conservative feedback level, and moderate wet/dry mix. Add tape
wobble, then bring shimmer up for one transition; lower feedback before changing
the delay time.

833 — BUZZYBEE: RECORDED XY SWARM
====================================================================
Patch: pitch sequence → pitch input; optional gate → Pulse In 1; BuzzyBee stereo
outs → mixer. Set a simple two- or three-note chord mode, then record a slow XY
path: corner, diagonal, brief pause, return. Play it back against the sequence.
Send a very slow LFO to pitch or timbre only after the XY movement is established.

84 — COSMIK C1ZZL3: TURING-MODULATED PHASE DISTORTION
====================================================================
Patch: Turing/generative CV source → pitch CV; related pulse → gate; Cosmik
C1ZZL3 audio out → mixer. Send a second related random CV to phase-distortion
amount or filter colour. Begin with Core as the stable voice; introduce the Rad
or Turing-oriented layer at low level, then use clocked probability for variation.

87 — FR330HFR33: ACID SEQUENCE WITH CONTROLLED OVERDRIVE
====================================================================
Patch: pitch sequencer → pitch CV; accented gate/trigger → gate input; audio out
→ mixer. Set saw or square, short decay, mid filter cutoff, and low distortion.
Use a second sequencer row or envelope for cutoff accents. Raise resonance until
the accent notes speak, then add drive only enough to thicken the loudest steps.

93 — TURING MATRIX: CLOCKED PROBABILITY MIXER
====================================================================
Patch: master clock → Turing Matrix clock; pitch CV → oscillator 1V/oct; pulse
out → envelope/drum trigger; additional CV/mixer out → filter cutoff; oscillator
→ VCA → mixer. Start with the loop probability high so a pattern repeats, then
lower it a little for drifting mutations. Use the mixer layer to crossfade stable
and unstable control streams during a performance.

999 — DUB WARNING: GATED SIREN SEND
====================================================================
Patch: gate or sparse trigger pattern → Pulse In 1; Dub Warning stereo outs →
mixer. Optional pitch/modulation CV → CV In 1, or audio/CV source → Audio In 1.
Begin in Gated mode with low effect amount. Use a small pitch sweep for each hit,
then increase delay/effect for the final bar; fire the Wheel Up gesture only as a
one-shot fill.

END OF PATCH NOTES
