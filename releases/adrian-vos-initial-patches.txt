ADRIAN VOS CARDS — INITIAL PATCHES

These are first-use starting points, distilled from the cards' READMEs and
info.yaml files. Start at low level and make one change at a time. Routes
named "to mixer" mean use the Workshop mixer inputs and monitor from its
main outputs.

101 C1ZZL3 GNARLY
Select Oscillator 1 and a recipe from its oscillator page. Keep shared pitch
near centre, then take Audio Out 1 to the mixer. Add only a gentle LFO to one
modulation input after the core sound is working.

118 JP8K SANDSQUALL
Use the documented Supersaw Lead initial setup. Start Voice A, bring in Voice B
with only slight detune, and send the audio output to the mixer. Increase spread
slowly once the basic lead is audible.

174 SKIPPING STONES
Choose Coupled mode with moderate density. Send CV Out 1 to oscillator pitch and
Pulse Out 1 to an envelope or Slopes trigger. Route the oscillator through a VCA
to the mixer; add the second output only when the first rhythm is reliable.

197 COSMIK M1N1
Start with the Filter Voice at modest resonance. Patch pitch CV and a gate to the
voice inputs, then route its audio output to the mixer. Keep the filter part-open
and leave external OSC3 disconnected until the basic bass voice works.

202 SCARYLINGO
On the Performance page, connect one sound source to Audio In 1 and route the
output to the mixer. Begin with carrier, mix and drive low. Increase ring-mod mix
only after confirming the dry input is heard.

29 XHT
Follow the Quick Guide's default sound. Connect pitch CV and a trigger if using
one, then route the stereo outputs to the mixer. Keep movement controls low and
add slow modulation only after a stable chord is audible.

34 DUALQUANT
Feed a short clean phrase to the audio input and route the output to the mixer.
Start with minimal granular shift and feedback. If using its quantised CV, patch
one output to oscillator pitch before trying the second.

43 CASTLE PROCESS
Put a simple oscillator or drum loop into the audio input and take the output to
the mixer. Select the middle mode, keep chopping low, and raise feedback only
after clean processing is confirmed.

433 SENSE OF SPACE
Begin in a quiet room with the output at a low level. Route the audio output to
the mixer and use a slow or manual trigger only if the card's restart input is
needed. Let the controls create occasional events rather than dense activity.

49 TAPE BIAS
Record a short mono phrase into the audio input and route the output to the
mixer. Set Bias around centre and make only a small adjustment during replay.
Keep transport triggering and CV disconnected for the first pass.

52 F1SHNET
Feed a steady, filtered sound or drum loop into the input and route its output to
the mixer. Set a stable filter response with low resonance. Add envelope following
or stepped modulation only once the unmodulated signal is clear.

54 TAPEGRADE
Send a mono sound to the input and use both outputs as a stereo pair into the
mixer. Start at neutral tape condition: nearly no wow/flutter and modest wet mix.
Introduce one movement control very slowly.

58 LOCHO VIBES
Feed a chord or sustained stereo source through LoCho Vibes and take the stereo
outputs to the mixer. Start in chorus with low rate and depth. Keep the effect
subtle before moving towards vibrato.

580 VORTEX RUNNER
On the oscillator page, start Voice A, then add Voice B with slight detune.
Route Audio Out 1 to one mixer channel and Audio Out 2 to a second channel.
Keep Voice B quieter until the two outputs are understood.

59 BITPHASE
Feed a drum loop, bass or held note into the audio input and route the output to
the mixer. Use a slow phaser setting and low resonance. Do not add tremolo or CV
until the phase sweep is clearly audible.

74 WILD PEBBLE
In Steady mode, connect the primary trigger/clock input. Send its quantised CV
to oscillator pitch and its pulse to an envelope or Slopes trigger. Route the
resulting voice to the mixer; keep generative variation low to start.

77 PUNK CONFUSION
Start in Atari Punk Console mode with a low output level and route the main audio
output to the mixer. A simple gate into Pulse In 1 supplies rhythm. Leave Broken
Venue and external audio for the second experiment.

818 BIB
Use the main page with low mix and feedback. Feed one sound into the input and
route the output to the mixer. Establish a short, clear delay first; add tape
motion and shimmer only after checking feedback remains controlled.

833 BUZZYBEE
Connect stable pitch and gate sources, then route its audio output to the mixer.
Set a simple sound before using XY. Record one short, slow XY gesture, listen to
it back, and only then add further modulation.

84 COSMIK C1ZZL3
Build a stable voice from Oscillator 1 Core and take Audio Out 1 to the mixer.
Use pitch CV and a related gate if available. Keep phase-distortion or Turing
modulation at zero for the first sound, then raise one amount gently.

87 FR330HFR33
Create a filtered voice with pitch and gate, then route its audio output to the
mixer. Begin with low drive and modest resonance. Add an accented cutoff control
only after the basic sequence speaks consistently.

93 TURING MATRIX
Follow the documented quickstart: clock one channel, patch its quantised CV to
oscillator pitch, and patch its gate to an envelope or Slopes input. Route that
oscillator through a VCA to the mixer. Begin with a repeating pattern before
adding probability changes.

999 DUB WARNING
Start in Gated mode. Feed Pulse In 1 from a sparse trigger, route both audio
outputs to two mixer channels, and keep effect level low. Add one pitch or CV
input only once the gated siren is working.
